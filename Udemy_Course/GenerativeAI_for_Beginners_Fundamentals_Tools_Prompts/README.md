### What is Generative AI?
- Generative AI is artificial intelligence that creates new content-  Text, images, audio, video, even code.
- The word generative means  It generates, it creates.It produces something new based on patterns it has learned.

### How It works?
- When you ask to write an email for you, it's not copying an email from somewhere else. It's generating a brand new email word by word, based on patterns it learned from millions of other texts. When you use an AI image generator to create a picture of a sunset over mountains, it's not finding an existing photo. It's creating a completely new image that has never existed before.

### Generative AI VS Traditional AI
- Traditional AI systems are designed to analyze, classify or predict. They take existing data and make sense of it. For example, a traditional AI might look at thousands of emails and classify them as spam or not spam. It's reading and categorizing. Another traditional AI might analyze sales data and predict next quarter's revenue. It's interpreting existing information.
- Generative AI does something entirely different. It doesn't just analyze. It creates. It doesn't just classify, it produces. It create originates new thing, original image, etc. It produce content that never existed.
- Example of Generative AI are autocomplete while writing, document summarizing, writing emails


### Types of Generative AI:

#### 1. Text Generation:
- Most recognized form of generative AI.
- Capabilities: Drafting emails, Writing blog posts, Summarizing reports, Translating languages, Coding
- Examples of tools: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini
- Importance: Relevant in communication
#### 2. Image Generation:
- AI systems create original images from text descriptions.
- Applications include concept art and product mockups.
- Improvement in image quality leading to photorealistic results.
- Concerns about copyright and ethical use.
#### 3. Audio and Video Generation:
- Audio AI: Produces human-like voices and music.
- Video Generation: Rapidly evolving, capable of creating short clips and synthetic videos.
- Challenges related to deepfakes and misinformation.
#### 4. Code Generation:
- AI writes computer code from natural language descriptions.
- Tools like GitHub Copilot assist developers by suggesting code.
- Enhances efficiency without replacing programmers.

##### Multimodal AI Systems: Capable of handling multiple types of content simultaneously. It can create audio and video at same time in same workflow.
